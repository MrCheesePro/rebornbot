#  Discord-linked minecraft console client bot

## What it contains:

| Name | Command | What it does |
|-------|---------|-------------|
| **link** | `!link` | Links your discord account to your minecraft account |
| **unlink** | `!unlink` | Unlinks your discord account from your minecraft account |
| **changelink** | `!changelink <ign>` | changes your link from your discord account to a new minecraft account |
| **leaderboard** | `!leaderboard or !lb` | shows the wallcheck leaderboard |
| **weewoo** | `!weewoo` | triggers a raid alert |
| **weeoff** | `!weeoff` | turns off raid alert |
| **stats** | `!stats or !stats <ign>` | shows your stats or another player's stats |
| **start** | `/!start` | start the wall check timer |
| **stop** | `!stop` | stops the wall check timer |
| **ftop** | `!ftop` | start the ftop timer |
| **ftopStop** | `!ftopstop` | stops the ftop timer |
| **tax goals** | `!tax goals <amount> <hours>` | Start a tax goal. Example: `!tax goal 1000000 24` (1 million goal, 24 hour duration) |
